Order of work

What the proof found, and what it changed (27 Nov, overnight)

The same questions asked live at the Ask panel, the coworker and an agent's door, beside the same model given only the schema and a read-only SQL tool. Read every pair on the Answer Pairs page; nothing there is graded by a model.

Where the platform lost, and the cause
  • Open questions read only the last 30 daystheLook redraws its recent days, so every figure was “still settling” and the answer had nothing to say; the plain model described eight years.
  • Earlier answers became the topic“The earlier response could not validate…” — each door found the one before it.
  • True figures withheld as untracedthe evidence the check read was cut before its rows by ~20k characters of SQL, and code-measured results counted only if cited.
  • A why split by segment, never volume × valuethe plain model's 18-second answer (orders −20%, value per order +19%) was the one the analyst missed in six minutes.
  • Hedges in place of findings“the evidence does not establish…”, asked for in the answer itself.
  • Doors re-measured governed metrics$2.33M (cancelled items counted) beside the governed $1.98M; agents' doors pinned “quick”, so a why from Teams never reached the analyst.
  • Declared words treated as unknown“YNAP” (a declared rule over four platforms) refused as “not present in this data” at every door; “VIP customers” (a declared rule) met with “What counts as vip?” — even at doors that cannot answer back; “What is GMV?” answered “no approved definition” beside the approved GMV metric.
  • Breakdowns answered a row per ordera repair regrouped “revenue by category” and “orders in each status” by order_id, and the shape check stood down; “reports in EUR” was read as an order to convert theLook's dollars.
What changed
  • An open question reads the data across its spanevery approved metric by year, quarter or month, its change and peak, and the mix by the curated breakdown — measured by code, cited as results; a topic reads its metrics, a named period its months.
  • Earlier answers only as a moved valuea failed or valueless one is no baseline.
  • Evidence keeps its rowsSQL capped in the evidence; code-measured results always count.
  • A why splits volume from value, then segment
  • The answer says findings; what it could not settle is a caveatheld in code, not only asked.
  • The kind decides at every dooragents' doors default to the kind's route; Teams is acknowledged at once and answered after.
  • Declared words are known everywhereone reading of what the declared ontology resolves serves the value probe and the clarify door; a door that cannot answer back is never asked; a definition falls back to the approved metric.
  • A breakdown keeps its graina named measure is an aggregate ask and “in each X” a grouping, so a repair that regroups by order is refused; the organisation's currency is said to convert nothing.
  • Alsoa prose stop is asked to conclude; “over the last year” is the year to yesterday; a reopened thread replays its envelope; Agent Ops shows a run by what it recorded; a metric's two readings are said, not chosen in silence.

Final run, commit 61fd800f, 07:20 IST: all 60 platform answers came back with a headline (10 questions × 3 doors × 2 connections), where at the start of the night an open question came back with nothing at all. Open questions now give the long-run story by the governed metrics — theLook's revenue up 720% from 2020 to 2025, Lux's fewer orders at a higher value with Mytheresa taking share — and the three doors agree on it. Declared definitions decide where they exist (YNAP = four platforms, VIP = VIP or Top Customer), so those answers differ from the plain model's guesses by design. Still behind the plain model: speed (medians 32–40 s at the doors, 6–14 s for it; a why takes 3–7 minutes), breadth on open questions (it reads tables no metric governs), and a why that names the volume/value split but still hedges on cause. Two fixes landed after this run (a false fan-out alarm, repeated cause caveats) and are live since 07:22.
